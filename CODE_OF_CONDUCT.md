# Code of conduct

KubeHabitat aims to be a respectful, welcoming technical community. Participants should be able to
contribute regardless of experience, background, identity, or disability.

## Expected behavior

- Communicate respectfully and assume good intent while addressing concrete impact.
- Give specific, constructive feedback and welcome correction.
- Respect other people's time, privacy, accessibility needs, and boundaries.
- Critique technical decisions and behavior without attacking people.
- Help newcomers find the relevant context and documentation.

Harassment, discriminatory abuse, threats, sexualized attention, doxxing, impersonation, and
repeated personal attacks are unacceptable. Do not publish private information without consent.

## Scope and reporting

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions when enabled, and community
spaces maintained for KubeHabitat. It also applies when representing the project publicly.

For visible misconduct in an issue or PR, ask the maintainer to moderate the conversation. For a
sensitive report, request a private reporting channel from the maintainer without posting the
incident's confidential details. A dedicated confidential contact is to be established as the
community grows.

Maintainers should protect reporters' privacy and review reports fairly. Depending on severity and
repetition, enforcement may include removing content, a warning, restricting participation, or a
temporary or permanent ban. Explain decisions privately when appropriate, and allow a factual
appeal.
