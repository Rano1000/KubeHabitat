# Visual assets

`demo-explorer.png`, `demo-inspector.png`, and `field-guide.png` are screenshots captured from the
implemented Phase 01 app. They display authored fixtures and are not screenshots of a live cluster.

KubeHabitat’s first visual identity was selected by the project maintainer during product planning.

| Asset                                        | Origin                                                         | Purpose                                                                              |
| -------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| `frontend/public/brand/kubehabitat-logo.png` | AI-generated project-specific logo approved by the maintainer  | First identity, combining Kubernetes-inspired wheel imagery with the habitat concept |
| `cluster-house-concept.png`                  | AI-generated project-specific concept approved during planning | Intended full product; not an implementation screenshot                              |
| `roadmap-concept.png`                        | AI-generated planning illustration                             | Reference artwork; the written roadmap is authoritative                              |
| `architecture.svg`                           | Original source-authored KubeHabitat diagram                   | Accessible target-architecture overview                                              |

`live-discovery.svg` is an original source-authored diagram of the implemented read-only discovery
and credential boundary.

Source-authored diagrams are covered by the repository’s Apache-2.0 license. Generated artwork is
available under the same license to the extent of the project’s rights. Third-party marks remain
subject to their owners’ rights. KubeHabitat is not an official Kubernetes project.
